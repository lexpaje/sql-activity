# Intermediate SQL Exercises

These exercises build on beginner concepts and introduce more complex SQL operations.

## Topics Covered
- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN / RIGHT JOIN
- Multiple JOINs
- GROUP BY with HAVING
- Subqueries
- String functions
- Date functions
- CASE statements

## Exercises

### Exercise 1: INNER JOIN
**Question:** List all employees with their department names.

### Exercise 2: JOIN with WHERE
**Question:** List all employees in the 'Engineering' department with their job titles.

### Exercise 3: Multiple JOINs
**Question:** List all order items with customer name, product name, and quantity.

### Exercise 4: LEFT JOIN
**Question:** List all departments and the count of employees in each (include departments with no employees).

### Exercise 5: Aggregate with JOIN
**Question:** Show each customer's name and their total order amount (sum of all orders).

### Exercise 6: GROUP BY with HAVING
**Question:** Find departments with an average salary greater than 70000.

### Exercise 7: Subquery in WHERE
**Question:** Find all employees who earn more than the average salary.

### Exercise 8: Subquery in SELECT
**Question:** List each employee with their salary and the difference from the average salary.

### Exercise 9: Correlated Subquery
**Question:** Find employees who earn more than the average salary in their own department.

### Exercise 10: EXISTS
**Question:** Find all customers who have placed at least one order.

### Exercise 11: CASE Statement
**Question:** Categorize employees' salaries as 'Low' (<60000), 'Medium' (60000-80000), or 'High' (>80000).

### Exercise 12: Date Functions
**Question:** Calculate how many years each employee has been with the company.

### Exercise 13: String Functions
**Question:** Create a full email display as "Last Name, First Name <email>" for all employees.

### Exercise 14: Self JOIN
**Question:** List each employee with their manager's name.

### Exercise 15: Complex GROUP BY
**Question:** Show total sales by product category, ordered by total sales descending.

### Exercise 16: Multiple Aggregates
**Question:** For each department, show the count of employees, average salary, and total salary cost.

### Exercise 17: JOIN with Aggregates
**Question:** List all projects with the number of employees assigned to each.

### Exercise 18: Date Range Query
**Question:** Find all orders placed in the last 6 months (from the most recent order date).

### Exercise 19: Subquery in FROM
**Question:** Find the department with the highest average salary.

### Exercise 20: Complex Filtering
**Question:** Find products that have never been ordered.

### Exercise 21: UNION
**Question:** Create a list of all people (employees and customers) with their contact info.

### Exercise 22: Advanced HAVING
**Question:** Find customers who have placed orders totaling more than $1000.

### Exercise 23: Multiple JOINs with Aggregates
**Question:** Show each customer's total spending by product category.

### Exercise 24: Date Extraction
**Question:** Show monthly order counts for 2023.

### Exercise 25: Nested Subqueries
**Question:** Find the employee(s) with the highest salary in each department.

## Tips for Success
- Draw out the table relationships before writing complex joins
- Use table aliases to make queries more readable
- Test subqueries independently before combining them
- Break complex queries into smaller parts
